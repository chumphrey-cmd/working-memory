## A Balanced Team Approach to Reliability

* https://labspractices.com/guides/reliability/

* Software reliability - software that is anti-fragile and benefits from strain on its system.
    * Includes concerns about availability, latency, security, and data durability, to regulatory requirements for auditing system interactions, to accessibility concerns, and more.

* From the beginning of a products inception, consider the following:
    * (1) articulate reliability outcomes in terms of user expectations and business needs
    * (2) prioritize outcomes by impact and likelihood so that the team has a good sense for where to start.

## Principles of Building Software

There's this interesting idea that came to mind after listening to lecture one of Clean Code by Robert Miller (Uncle Bob). He spoke around the idea of professionalizing the field of software development similar to how other disciplines (e.g., medicine, manufacturing, law, etc.) have established a code of ethics. The role of someone paid to build and **craft** software is very nascent when compared to other long-standing professions and serves as an interesting thought experiment... Below are some principles that may serve as a good starting point and mental model for myself when thinking about building really excellent software.

* Lean-Agile
* XP
* UCD
* Balanced Teams
* Speed to Value
